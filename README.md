# Growth Audit — Timeleft

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/timeleft-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Timeleft already has. Each one comes with a clickable prototype of Timeleft's onboarding and paywall, rebuilt in Timeleft's own design. Only the order of the screens changed. The look did not.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Put the dinner they just picked on the paywall | Paywall + 20%-off sheet | CR +15–20% |
| 02 | Make your three prices readable | Paywall | CR +5–8% |
| 03 | Ask for the account after the paywall | Account | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 04 | Ask for location and notifications once they've paid | City + Notifications | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Say their answers back while the loader runs | Loader | CR +10–15% |

## Grounding

Every price, rating, review and figure comes from Timeleft's own published material: the App Store listing, timeleft.com and the in-app screens. Prices are the HUF prices shown on the live iOS paywall and its 20%-off sheet.

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps, not measured lift for Timeleft.

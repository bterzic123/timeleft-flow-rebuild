What this is

We walked your onboarding the way a new user does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion and ARPU across the market, not just inside one app. We pointed that at Timeleft. We installed it, went through the whole flow up to the paywall and its 20%-off sheet, read your App Store listing, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you show people.

What follows is five ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours: the cream surfaces, the black pill buttons, the orange-to-pink progress bar, the energy slider, your serif paywall headline. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already have but show in the wrong place.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If you have already run one of these, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
